Bigger models give better answers and cost more to run. If you're not sure, Large is the most common choice for a serious assistant.

2How much use will it get?

This sets both the hours you pay for and the traffic the machines must keep up with — usually the biggest lever on the bill.

3What matters most to you?

Lowest cost accepts machines that can be interrupted and a compressed model. Reliability commits for a year and adds a managed platform.
